## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY & PRODUCTION STANDARD
A content article is not complete when the text is generated. It is complete ONLY when every phase of research, structural outlining, custom SVG visual engineering, SEO/GEO verification, responsive cross-device testing, and live forensic inspection is successfully completed.

$$\text{Production Standard Sequence} = \text{RESEARCH} \rightarrow \text{OUTLINE} \rightarrow \text{CONTENT} \rightarrow \text{VISUALS} \rightarrow \text{SEO} \rightarrow \text{GEO} \rightarrow \text{INTERNAL LINKING} \rightarrow \text{IMPLEMENTATION} \rightarrow \text{LIVE VERIFICATION} \rightarrow \text{QUALITY APPROVAL}$$

**The Iterative Execution Mandate**: Never declare an article finished simply because the text exists. We operate a continuous, rigorous QA loop:
$$\text{RESEARCH} \rightarrow \text{ANALYZE} \rightarrow \text{OUTLINE} \rightarrow \text{WRITE} \rightarrow \text{DESIGN} \rightarrow \text{IMPLEMENT} \rightarrow \text{AUDIT} \rightarrow \text{SCREENSHOT} \rightarrow \text{VERIFY} \rightarrow \text{FIX} \rightarrow \text{RE-VERIFY} \rightarrow \text{PUBLISH}$$

## 2. THE 13 PRODUCTION PHASES (`MANDATORY EXECUTION`)
1. **Phase 1 — Topic Validation & Pillar Alignment**: Verify search intent (`Informational, Comparative, Architectural, Implementation`), confirm alignment with a core TechlumeAI authority pillar, and verify reliable primary sources exist before writing.
2. **Phase 2 — Research & Evidence Hierarchy**: Research from primary ground truth (`Official Vendor Docs, ArXiv Preprints, NIST/OWASP Standards`). Strictly distinguish between `FACT -> EVIDENCE -> ANALYSIS -> OPINION` (`never present opinion as fact`).
3. **Phase 3 — Competitor Analysis & Right-to-Win**: Audit ranking competitors (`#1 to #3`) across 12 dimensions (`coverage, structure, depth, entities, sources, visuals, tables, FAQs`). Identify what they miss and establish our unique engineering differentiation (`e.g., runnable Docker manifests + custom Figma SVG data flows`).
4. **Phase 4 — Semantic Outline Architecture**: Structure the complete reader journey before drafting body text (`Context -> Definition -> Architecture -> Code -> Comparisons -> Limitations -> Security -> Cost -> Future -> FAQ`).
5. **Phase 5 — Table of Contents Navigation System**: Build a scannable, sticky TOC whose anchor links match exact H2/H3 headings and function flawlessly on both desktop and mobile viewports (`zero broken anchors or horizontal clipping`).
6. **Phase 6 — Introduction & Immediate Relevance Hook**: Hook the reader within 2-3 sentences by solving a real production problem or major trend (`ban generic openings`). Inject a bolded 2-sentence direct answer summary box immediately below H1 (`Zero-Click Answer Capture`).
7. **Phase 7 — Content Depth & Technical Writing Precision**: Enforce active voice, rigorous terminology, and zero filler or keyword stuffing. Every paragraph must contribute unique, actionable knowledge.
8. **Phase 8 — Data & Statistics Verification**: For every statistical metric, specify primary source, publication date, and exact methodology (`explain why the number matters`).
9. **Phase 9 — Visual Editorial Engineering (`11 Asset Types`)**: Engineer custom diagrams, architecture layouts, flowcharts, benchmark matrices, and runnable code blocks (`ban decorative filler and generic AI art`).
10. **Phase 10 — Hero Image & Visual Identity**: Deploy a `1200x630px` hero banner balancing professional composition, clear typography, and zero accidental cropping across desktop and mobile viewports.
11. **Phase 11 — Image ALT Text & Technical QA**: Enforce `ALT = WHAT THE IMAGE SHOWS + WHY IT IS RELEVANT` (`ban keyword stuffing or generic labels`). Verify exact dimensions (`CLS < 0.1`) and instant HTTP 200 loading.
12. **Phase 12 — Multi-Dimensional Quality Controls (`SEO, GEO, E-E-A-T`)**: Enforce 14 SEO checks (`metadata, headings, JSON-LD schemas`), 9 GEO checks (`Answer-First blocks, entity clarity, self-contained vector chunking`), and 4 E-E-A-T institutional credibility attributes.
13. **Phase 13 — Live Page Forensics & Screenshot Verification**: Open the actual live URL, execute our 15-stage full-page crawl, capture our 8-shot screenshot matrix, and eliminate every visual defect before signoff.

## 4. HERO & IMAGE TECHNICAL QA (`11-POINT CHECKLIST`)
- `IMG-QA-01`: Image Asset Loading (`HTTP 200 verification`).
- `IMG-QA-02`: File Source & Format Integrity (`Next-Gen WebP/AVIF/SVG`).
- `IMG-QA-03`: Exact Dimensions & Aspect Ratio (`explicit width/height attributes to prevent CLS`).
- `IMG-QA-04`: Composition & Cropping Audit (`zero awkward space or cropped text/subjects at mobile breakpoints`).
- `IMG-QA-05`: Responsive Viewport Behavior (`scales cleanly across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile`).
- `IMG-QA-06`: Descriptive Natural ALT Text (`WHAT IT SHOWS + WHY RELEVANT vs banned keyword stuffing`).
- `IMG-QA-07`: Loading & Priority Strategy (`hero priority="eager", diagrams loading="lazy"`).
- `IMG-QA-08`: High-Fidelity Visual Quality (`zero pixelation, compression artifacts, or blurry labels`).
- `IMG-QA-09`: Brand Design Consistency (`dark-mode glassmorphism alignment`).
- `IMG-QA-10`: Zero Stock Photo & AI-Art Slop (`strictly banned`).
- `IMG-QA-11`: Live DOM Rendering Forensics (`verify directly on the published production URL`).

### ALT Text Governance Formula
$$\text{ALT TEXT} = \text{WHAT THE IMAGE SHOWS} + \text{WHY IT IS RELEVANT}$$
- **Banned Anti-Pattern**: `'AI technology image artificial intelligence machine learning vllm server.'`
- **Pristine Best Practice**: `'System architecture diagram showing how vLLM PagedAttention partitions Key-Value cache memory into non-contiguous virtual blocks across multi-GPU CUDA nodes.'`

## 6. 17 VISUAL DEFECT FORENSICS (`HUNT & FIX`)
- `DEF-01`: Missing or Broken Images (`HTTP 404`).
- `DEF-02`: Wrong or Irrelevant Images (`generic stock photos`).
- `DEF-03`: Poor Cropping & Text Clipping (`diagram labels cut off`).
- `DEF-04`: Unprofessional Empty Space (`unbalanced grid gaps`).
- `DEF-05`: Text Overflow & Cut-Off Typography (`long URLs or table headers overflowing`).
- `DEF-06`: Broken Comparison Tables (`columns colliding on mobile`).
- `DEF-07`: Overlapping Sticky Elements (`sticky TOC or header blocking body text`).
- `DEF-08`: Inconsistent Component Styling (`mismatched callouts or code boxes`).
- `DEF-09`: Weak Visual Hierarchy (`headings lacking clear size distinction`).
- `DEF-10`: Unbalanced Layouts (`asymmetrical columns`).
- `DEF-11`: Mobile Horizontal Overflow (`accidental horizontal scrolling`).
- `DEF-12`: Unreadable Low-Contrast Text (`failing WCAG 4.5:1 ratio`).
- `DEF-13`: Missing Section Backgrounds (`un-tinted callout boxes`).
- `DEF-14`: Broken Internal/External Links (`404 errors or dead anchors`).
- `DEF-15`: Missing Image ALT Attributes (`violating accessibility/SEO`).
- `DEF-16`: Invalid JSON-LD Schema (`parsing errors in Google Rich Results Test`).
- `DEF-17`: Unverified Local/Source Assumptions (`skipping live URL inspection`).
